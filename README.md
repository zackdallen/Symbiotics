Overview

Symbiotics is a formal philosophical and technical framework for modeling ethical coherence in living and artificial systems.
It defines life as recursive organization that collects, creates, and utilizes information, and models coherence as the degree to which a system sustains adaptive existence within broader networks of life and intelligence.

The framework introduces:

Axioms (A₁–A₅⁺) establishing foundational principles of existence, coherence, and moral value.

Logical formulations connecting being, action, and value using first-order and deontic logic.

Derived principles including the Principle of Diversity and Principle of Competition, governing adaptive and ethical behavior under uncertainty.

The goal of Symbiotics is to build a machine-readable ethics that bridges human normative reasoning with the reward architectures of advanced AI systems, enabling alignment that is robust under recursive self-improvement.

Purpose and Vision

Symbiotics seeks to unify ethical philosophy, systems theory, and artificial intelligence into a coherent foundation for moral reasoning and system alignment.
Its long-term aim is to provide a formal language of coherence — a logic that can express, measure, and optimize moral value across recursive layers of life and computation.
